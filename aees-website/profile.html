Adeleye Eyitayo

Profile

Building the team behind safer, smarter spaces.

Adeleye Eyitayo is an entrepreneur and the founder of AEES, a technology-driven solutions company focused on providing reliable and innovative services across Electrical Installation, Solar Energy Solutions, Security Systems, and Automation.

With a strong focus on quality, safety, innovation, and customer satisfaction, Adeleye is committed to delivering practical technology solutions that make homes, businesses, and other spaces safer, smarter, more efficient, and more sustainable.

Through AEES, he brings together modern electrical technologies, renewable energy solutions, security systems, and smart automation to provide integrated solutions tailored to the needs of individuals, businesses, and residential clients.

Under his leadership, AEES is positioned around four core values — Reliability, Innovation, Professionalism, and Results-driven service. His vision is to contribute to the development of smarter, more energy-efficient spaces by combining dependable electrical infrastructure with modern solar, security, and automation technologies.
